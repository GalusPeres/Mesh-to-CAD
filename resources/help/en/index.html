Mesh-to-CAD help

These pages describe the tools of Mesh-to-CAD. In the application, F1 opens the page of the active tool.

Getting started From the bundled example scan to a STEP file.

Tools

File

  • Import scan

Prepare

  • Brush
  • Lasso
  • Rectangle
  • Smart select
  • Reduce Lowers the number of triangles.
  • Delete selection Deletes the selected triangles from the scan.
  • Fill holes Closes small holes in the scan.
  • Remove small parts Removes loose parts such as turntable remains or dust.
  • Repair Welds duplicate vertices, removes broken triangles and makes the orientation consistent.
  • Smooth Smooths how the scan is displayed.
  • Scan information Shows the size and condition of the scan.

Align

  • Segment
  • Align automatically Largest plane on XY, largest plane perpendicular to it on XZ, origin at the corner of the scan.
  • Align to faces Alignment to fitted faces, axes or regions by the 3-2-1 principle.
  • Reset alignment Show the scan in its original coordinates again.

Model

  • Fit shape
  • Reference geometry
  • Sketch Section of the scan as a sketch of lines, arcs and circles
  • Combine
  • Extrude
  • Fillet
  • Primitive
  • Revolve
  • Split body
  • Freeform patch Fit a B-spline surface to a region of the scan, for example to split a body.
  • Loft Body through sections of the scan along an axis.

Inspect

  • Deviation Compare the scan with the bodies in colour
  • Measure Measure distances, angles and diameters
  • Report
  • Export STEP … Save bodies as a STEP file
  • Export STL … Save bodies as an STL file

Mouse

Orbit Right drag; drag on the view cube
Pan Middle drag, Shift + right drag
Zoom Wheel (to the cursor), Ctrl + right drag
Fit all Middle double-click
Context menu Right click without dragging
